Pages, forms and small apps for dealerships, gyms and local businesses. Three are live; the rest I can walk you through.

  1. 01 Dream Wheels

    Golden Mile Chrysler · Dream Wheels on Eglinton Fest 2026

    A booth raffle for the 4th annual street festival, in support of SickKids: scan a QR code, enter to win free oil for life. One self-contained HTML file, no framework or build step, talking to Firestore over REST, with every entry also written to device storage so submissions survive dead venue wifi. Ships with a printable QR generator, an admin dashboard and a winner-draw script.

  2. Licence ScannerPDF417 + OCR · React · Express · Stripe

    02 Licence Scanner

    Dealership customer intake

    Captures a customer's driver's licence on a phone and turns it into a structured record, replacing the photocopier at the sales desk. Decodes the PDF417 barcode in the browser via WebAssembly, falls back to on-device OCR, exports to PDF. An npm-workspaces monorepo: React + Vite client, Express + Prisma + PostgreSQL server, Stripe billing, tested on real phones over a Cloudflare Tunnel.

    React 18TypeScriptPrismaStripeWebAssemblyOCR
    Private repository · walkthrough on request
