My work

Pages, forms and small apps for dealerships and local businesses, most of them for Golden Mile Chrysler. Every picture here is the real page, recorded as it scrolls. Three are live; the rest I can walk you through.

  4. 04 TTC Employee Pricing

    Golden Mile Chrysler · Promo landing section

    A promotional section for the dealership's TTC employee pricing on Dodge, Jeep and Ram, plus fifteen percent off service. Shipped as a drop-in block scoped to embed in the existing D2C Media Bootstrap site, a standalone preview, and a booking page with a TTC employee verification field, vehicle selector, date and time picker and validation.

  5. 05 Dream Wheels

    Golden Mile Chrysler · Dream Wheels on Eglinton Fest 2026

    A booth raffle for the 4th annual street festival, in support of SickKids: scan a QR code, enter to win free oil for life. One self-contained HTML file, no framework or build step, talking to Firestore over REST, with every entry also written to device storage so submissions survive dead venue wifi. Ships with a printable QR generator, an admin dashboard and a winner-draw script.

    HTMLJavaScriptFirestoreOffline-resilient
    GitHub ↗
