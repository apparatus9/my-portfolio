Dealership customer intake

Licence Scanner

Captures a customer's driver's licence on a phone and turns it into a structured record — replacing the photocopier at the sales desk. Decodes the PDF417 barcode in the browser via WebAssembly, falls back to on-device OCR, exports to PDF. An npm-workspaces monorepo: React + Vite client, Express + Prisma + PostgreSQL server, Stripe billing, tested on real phones over a Cloudflare Tunnel.

About

The developer behind the work

I build the unglamorous things that make a business money.

Most of my work lives in the gap between a business having a website and that website doing something useful. A dealership has a flyer for a promotion but nowhere to send people. A gym has a signup page that loses half the people who reach it. A team spends an afternoon photocopying driver's licences.

I take those problems and ship something that fixes them — often in days rather than months, and often designed to slot into whatever platform the business is already stuck with.

I care a lot about work holding up in the real world: forms that still capture entries when the venue wifi dies, pages that load fast on an old phone in a parking lot, and code the next developer can actually read.
