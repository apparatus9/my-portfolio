How a project goes

  1. 01

    01 Hello

    You tell me the goal. A flyer with nowhere to send people, a form that loses leads, a process stuck on paper. One call, and I'll tell you honestly whether I'm the right person for it, roughly what it takes, and how long it'll be.

  2. 02

    02 Plan

    We agree on what the page has to do, and what it doesn't. One clear action for the visitor. I look at what you already have so we build on it instead of around it.

  3. 03

    03 Build

    You get a live preview link almost from the start. Open it on your phone, tell me what's wrong, watch it change. No big reveal at the end.

  4. 04

    04 Launch

    It goes out on Vercel, on your domain, with submissions landing in your inbox. I check it on a slow phone and bad wifi, because that's where your customers are.

  5. 05

    05 After

    Everything documented for whoever comes next, in code the next developer can actually read. If something breaks, tell me. If you want more, we talk again.
